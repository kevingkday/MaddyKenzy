Itsy & Sleepy Kart

A kart game driven by one joystick. Push the stick to go, tilt it to steer. Every button on the joystick just honks the horn, and there is no pause screen or menu to fall into.

Looking for a joystick. Wiggle it or press any button on it so the browser can see it.

On an iPad or phone: drag the big yellow circle to drive (up to go, sideways to steer) and tap BEEP to honk. To leave, hold the button in the top corner.

The game goes fullscreen. Press Esc to leave it. To pick a world, tilt the stick toward a picture and hold. Arrow keys also drive, for testing without a joystick.

This browser could not start the 3D game. Try a current version of Chrome or Edge on a computer.
