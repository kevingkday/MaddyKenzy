Game 1

Itsy's Big Show

A purple spider hosts a show of quick, noisy, rewarding mini-games for letters, sounds, numbers and talking. Built for kids who drift after a couple of taps: every touch answers back, and every right answer gets a reward.

  • Ages about 3 to 7
  • iPad · iPhone · computer
  • No reading needed
▶ Play Itsy's Big ShowInstall it

How it plays

Seven activities, one tap each.

It opens with a big TV-show style intro, then "Who's playing?" (each child taps their own letter). The home screen puts the child's favourite activity first as a big pulsing tile, and that order keeps updating from how they actually play.

Bubble Pop

Hook · words · letters · numbers

Bubbles float up with pictures, letters and numbers. Each pop plays a note and says the word. Every 8 pops brings a surprise.

Say It!

Talking · making sounds

A picture and a word, then "Can you say moo?" Any sound the child makes counts: Itsy jumps and sparkles. If they stay quiet, Itsy says it again and moves on gently.

Spider Climb

Number order · counting up and down

"3, 4, hmm, 6. Which number is missing?" Itsy climbs the water spout with every right answer.

Ladybug Friends

+1 · −1 · +0 · −0

Ladybugs fly onto Itsy's web or fly away, then "How many friends now?" Adding zero is shown as nothing happening.

Letter Sounds

Letter names · letter sounds

"Octopus! aw, aw, octopus. Which letter makes that sound?" Letters a child misses come back more often.

ABC Board & Sing with Itsy

Free play · songs

Tap any letter to hear its name, sound and a word, or sing the ABCs as the letters light up. Sing with Itsy plays the whole Itsy Bitsy Spider with rain, sun and a climbing spider.

Talks and listens

A voice they know, and a game that hears them.

Every question, letter sound, number and "You did it!" plays in a parent's own voice, cloned once with ElevenLabs. That's about 450 short clips, saved on the device so they work offline.

The game listens too. In Say It!, the microphone only measures how loud the sound is, on the device itself. Nothing is recorded, stored or sent. A babble counts as much as a word, because the goal is to make trying feel great.

Built to hold attention

  • Every tap answers back with a sparkle and a note, even on empty space.
  • Short rounds, then a surprise reward: a line of the song, a new sticker friend or confetti.
  • If attention drifts (no taps, or fast random tapping), Itsy peeks in with a jingle and the right answer glows.
  • After a wrong answer, the right one lights up, so they learn from the miss without failing.
  • Difficulty adjusts itself to keep them succeeding about 80% of the time.

How it learns

What it notices, and what it can change.

What it records

Which activities each child opens and for how long, right and wrong answers and how fast, sounds made in Say It!, bubbles popped, rewards seen, levels reached, and signs of lost interest. It never records audio, and nothing typed by the child.

Notes about each child are encrypted per family. More on privacy.

What a review can change

SettingFor example
Activity orderPut the favourite first, hide one that's ignored
Round length2 answers instead of 3, for faster rewards
Reward mixMore song lines, fewer stickers
Words & promptsNew bubble pictures and "say it" words
PacingBubble speed, when Itsy peeks in

Every change is offered to a parent first, tested, and measured afterwards.

Install Itsy's Big Show

iPad & iPhone

  1. Open the game in Safari.
  2. Tap Share.
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen.

Android

  1. Open the game in Chrome.
  2. Tap ⋮.
  3. Tap Install app.

Grown-ups

Hold the ★ for 2 seconds to open the grown-ups screen: suggestions, voice, play timer and levels. Leaving any activity needs a press-and-hold.
