Each game is a standalone Progressive Web App (PWA). Once opened, it works full-screen and offline!

iPad & iPhone (Safari)

  1. Tap Play Game to open the game.
  2. Tap the Share button (square with an arrow).
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen.

Android & Chromebook (Chrome)

  1. Tap Play Game to open the game.
  2. Tap the menu (⋮) or install icon in the address bar.
  3. Choose Install app or Add to Home screen.
